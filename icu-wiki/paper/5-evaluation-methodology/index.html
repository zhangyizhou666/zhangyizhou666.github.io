5. Evaluation Methodology

Section question

Under the university QA situation, defining what should count as a reliable answer is important.

What counts as a reliable answer?Limits of standard RAG evaluationG-Eval-inspired answer-quality evaluationWorking reliability requirementBlocking cases

We propose four criteria that both the model and the retriever need to meet: Evidence-based, Summarize instead of copied, Citation useful, and No blocking case.

At first, we used RAGAS as a mainstream evaluation method to evaluate the whole-system RAG performance.

We then fed all answers to our judges and let them blindly evaluate the answers. The judges gave scores from 0 to 2 on four dimensions: answer acceptance, readability, citation usefulness, and over-answering risk.

Therefore, in our definition, a workable model condition needs to meet the 0.8 quality threshold and also have zero blocking cases.
